# Phase 2 seed sources

Last reviewed: 31 July 2026.

The seed is a discovery catalogue, not a live source of safety, permit, quota,
weather, or volcano-alert information. Visitors must check the relevant park
manager and Indonesian geological authority before travel. Elevations and
coordinates can differ between a volcanic complex, a named cone, and a hiking
summit; the values below follow the cited profile or named subfeature.

Elevation and coordinates primarily use the Smithsonian Institution Global
Volcanism Program (GVP):

- [Rinjani](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=264030)

Province, island, and landscape descriptions were cross-checked against the
same profiles and official destination or park descriptions where available.

## Editorial fields

`difficulty`, `beginner_score`, `duration_days`, `budget_category`,
`best_season`, `sunrise_rating`, and `popularity_score` are Jejak Puncak
editorial discovery attributes, not measurements issued by the cited
authorities. They are intentionally conservative and should be reviewed with
real product research before launch.

Hero image URLs for 11 mountains have been updated with location-verified
Unsplash photography. Detailed photographer credits, photo IDs, and direct URLs
are documented in `supabase/PHOTO_SOURCES.md`. Gunung Tambora remains on the
placeholder illustration until a location-verified photo is confirmed.
